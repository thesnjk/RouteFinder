# Competitor Pricing — August 2026

Baseline: **RouteFinder $0** (ORS free tier + optional TomTom/OpenWeather keys; no per-seat SaaS).

Prices from public App Store / e-shop / product pages (Aug 2026). Fleet products are **quote-only** unless noted.

## Pricing wedge for sales

1. **Owner-op:** "Same constraint routing class as Sygic at $0 — plus physics rehearsal Sygic doesn't have."
2. **Small fleet:** "CoPilot Account Manager per-seat vs RouteFinder LAN dispatch + trip brief on hardware you already own."
3. **Honest limit:** "We don't include TRAVIS booking or remote VU — you keep your telematics vendor for that."
