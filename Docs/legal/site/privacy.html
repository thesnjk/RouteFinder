1. Scope

This policy describes how RouteFinder processes personal data when you use RouteFinder iOS and macOS apps, RouteFinderFleetServer on a LAN, the web dispatch console, the Android fleet driver app, an optional hosted gateway VPS, and pilot programmes under a signed pilot agreement.

RouteFinder is primarily an on-device / on-LAN product. Default fleet trip and inspection data stay on Operator devices and the Operator’s LAN (or Operator-controlled hosted gateway) store.

3. Lawful bases (UK GDPR)

  • Contract — provide routing, dispatch, and pilot services you request
  • Legitimate interests — product security, abuse prevention, improving advisory features
  • Consent — where you tick privacy consent at signup, or optional analytics (if introduced later)
  • Legal obligation — where required by law

4. Third-party processors / APIs

When API keys or the fleet / hosted ORS/Pelias proxy are enabled, requests may go to HeiGIT OpenRouteService, optional TomTom / OpenWeather / WeatherKit, OpenStreetMap Overpass, map tile providers, and parking partner sites (TRAVIS / SNAP) when the driver opens deep links. Their terms apply. Apple App Store / Google Play process distribution under their policies.

5. Retention

  • Local account and settings: until you delete the app or clear Keychain / Keystore data
  • LAN / hosted fleet store: controlled by the Operator
  • Pilot correspondence: retained as needed, then deleted or anonymised

6. Your rights (UK)

You may request access, rectification, erasure, restriction, portability, and object to certain processing. Contact the privacy email above. You may complain to the ICO.

7. Children

RouteFinder is intended for professional drivers and fleet operators, not for children under 16.

8. International transfers

Default LAN keeps data on Operator networks. Remote URL / TLS or a hosted gateway is Operator’s hosting choice and risk.

9. Changes

We will update the “Last updated” date and, for material changes, notify in-app or by email to pilot contacts.

10. Driver Terms relationship

In-app Driver Terms cover routing liability and advisory use. This Privacy Policy covers personal data. Both apply.
